About This Book

A man wakes up to find himself transformed into a giant insect. Franz Kafka's masterpiece of modernist literature follows Gregor Samsa, a traveling salesman who awakens to discover he has been transformed into a monstrous vermin. This absurdist novella explores themes of alienation, identity, guilt, and the absurdity of existence, making it one of the most influential works of 20th-century literature.
